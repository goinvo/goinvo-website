New Topic

Sustainability encompasses the ability to maintain resources for present and future generations, extending beyond natural resources to include societal and economic resources as well.  It is often referred to as ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) or "planet, people, and profit".

Key Aspects of Sustainability/ESG:

Environmental (E):  Focuses on a business's impact on the environment. Examples include:

  • Carbon Emissions
  • Pollution and recycling
  • Waste management
  • Energy use
  • Deforestation
  • Climate change

Social (S):  Addresses how a business treats its employees, customers, and communities. Examples include:

  • Diversity and inclusion
  • HR policies
  • Human rights
  • Safety and labor standards
  • Privacy and customer care
  • Charitable work
  • Fair treatment of employees and working conditions
  • Support for local communities
