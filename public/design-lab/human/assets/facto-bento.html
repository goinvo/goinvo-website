Themes

Across generations, there is a shared concern about sustainability, health, and ethical consumption when it comes to choosing pet food. Gen Z consumers struggle with the environmental impact of single-use plastics and peer pressure, seeking brands that communicate their eco-friendly practices clearly. Millennials are highly aware of environmental challenges and value transparency and authenticity, balancing convenience with making sustainable choices. Boomers are increasingly conscious of leaving a better planet for future generations, prioritizing health and showing skepticism towards brands’ sustainability claims. All generations appreciate brands that simplify sustainable decision-making through clear communication and packaging options.

As a leader in the footwear industry, we are acutely aware of the environmental challenges posed by our sector and are committed to reducing our footprint while driving innovation in sustainable materials and practices. Billions of shoes end up in landfills yearly, taking hundreds of even thousands of years to decompose. Many shoes contain non-biodegradable plastics, including polyurethane and synthetic rubber, which significantly contribute to global waste and environmental degradation. This reality underscores the urgent need for transformative action and a shift towards a circular economy within the footwear industry.

In response to these challenges, we have implemented a comprehensive strategy aimed at reducing our environmental impact. Our approach focuses on sustainable design, circular economy initiatives, and manufacturing innovations. By 2026, we aim to incorporate biodegradable and recycled materials into 70% of our product lines. This year, we launched a take-back program that allows customers to return worn shoes, which are then recycled or upcycled into new products, ensuring that fewer shoes end up in landfills. Additionally, we are reducing our reliance on virgin plastics by 50% and integrating bio-based alternatives into our production processes.
